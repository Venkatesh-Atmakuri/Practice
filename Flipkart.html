Trees are one of the most precious gifts of nature. They give us clean air to breathe, provide shade, and help maintain the balance of the environment. A tree supports many forms of life—birds, insects, and animals all depend on it for food and shelter. Trees also reduce pollution, control soil erosion, and keep our surroundings cool. Planting and protecting trees is important not only for nature but also for the future of our planet. A single tree may seem small, but together, trees make the world a healthier and more beautiful place.
